"Before you send" checklist: who does each line, state by state
==============================================================

The rule: a line is left for a person only when no system could do it.
No lawyer has reviewed this. The long version is state-checklist-review.txt.

Three kinds of line:
  SYSTEM DOES IT     Apex prints the notice in the contract (copied from the official wording on
                     file, never retyped), in the place and the type the law states, with its
                     blanks filled; collects a separate signature or initials inside the
                     customer's own signing visit; builds the subcontractor and supplier list;
                     puts a required sentence beside the message; or gives the document to the
                     customer with the contract.
  SYSTEM CAN SEE IT  a person does it inside Apex (a signature, the photo acknowledgment) and
                     Apex ticks the line when it happens.
  PERSON ONLY        nothing in Apex can do or see it. The person ticks it by hand.

The counts are the most lines a state can show (a pool job sold at the customer's home, no license
number on file, the customer married, the sale made in English). A real contract shows fewer.
Lines that depend on one fact (homestead, married, credit, age, subcontractors) ask one Yes / No /
Not sure question; a No takes the line off the card.
A line that states a fact or asks the customer for a signature is done by the system only after a
Yes; the counts assume those Yes answers, the insurance questions answered in the contract
settings, and the project's subcontractors and suppliers on file with address and telephone.
The agency documents (pamphlets and forms that must be handed over unaltered) are counted as not
loaded yet: their lines show under PERSON until Apex staff load each file.

All states together: 118 system does it, 99 system can see it, 369 person only.

WAITING FOR THE AGENCY'S OWN DOCUMENT (12)
--------------------------------------------
The system is built to hand the agency's file to the customer before they sign, record the delivery
and collect the signed acknowledgment where the law asks for one. The file itself is not loaded yet.
Until Apex staff load it, the line stays with the contractor (who is not shown this note).

- Arizona: Hand the customer the state pool safety notice. The agency's file: AZ-pool-notice.
- Delaware: Attach the Attorney General's "Summary of Your Rights" and hand it over before the
  customer signs. The agency's file: DE-ag-summary.
- Illinois: Hand the customer the "Home Repair: Know Your Consumer Rights" pamphlet. The
  agency's file: IL-pamphlet.
- Illinois: Get the Consumer Rights Acknowledgment Form signed by you and the customer. The
  agency's file: IL-pamphlet.
- Maine: Attach the Attorney General's consumer information addendum. The agency's file: ME-ag.
- New Mexico: Give the state's residential default disclosure form before work, signing or
  payment, and get it signed. The agency's file: NM-default.
- Oregon: Give the owner the CCB "Information Notice to Owner About Construction Lien Rights" on
  or before the contract date. The agency's file: OR-lien.
- Oregon: Give the owner the CCB Consumer Protection Notice and get it signed. The agency's
  file: OR-ccb.
- Rhode Island: Attach the Board's consumer disclosures and the Summary of Registration Law. The
  agency's file: RI-board.
- Virginia: Give the customer the DPOR Statement of Consumer Protections and get a signed
  acknowledgment. The agency's file: VA-dpor.
- Washington: Hand over L&I's construction lien information with the Notice to Customer. The
  agency's file: WA-lien-info.
- Wisconsin: For building or remodeling a home, give the construction defect notice with the
  state brochure before signing. The agency's file: WI-brochure.


STAYS WITH A PERSON FOR GOOD: OUTSIDE APEX (5)
-------------------------------------------------
No system could do these: they happen on a document Apex does not make, on a state's own website,
or before a notary.

- Arizona: If the customer signs a promissory note, put the required statement on the face of
  the note. Why: The statement goes on the promissory note. Apex does not make promissory notes,
  so only a person can put it there.
- Connecticut: If the customer signs a promissory note, put the required statement on the face
  of the note. Why: The statement goes on the promissory note. Apex does not make promissory
  notes, so only a person can put it there.
- Illinois: Give the owner your sworn statement of everyone furnishing labor or materials before
  the first payment. Why: A sworn statement is sworn by a person before a notary.
- Iowa: Post the job on the state's mechanics lien registry within 10 days. Why: The registry is
  the state's own website; only a person with the contractor's account can post there.
- Wisconsin: If the customer signs a promissory note, put the required legend on the note. Why:
  The statement goes on the promissory note. Apex does not make promissory notes, so only a person
  can put it there.
