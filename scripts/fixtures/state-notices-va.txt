Format: Handed to the consumer with the contract; the contract carries the consumer's signed acknowledgment of having been provided with and read it; the form may be reproduced but not altered.
- [warn] Virginia requires this notice: Virginia Contractor Transaction Recovery Fund statement (18VAC50-22-260 B 9 k). The exact wording is not loaded yet. Get it from the official source or your attorney and attach it before the customer signs. When: Residential contracting (excluding subcontractors and routine maintenance or service contracts). Format: A statement in the contract that the Recovery Fund exists, with how to contact the board for claim information.
