Text Messaging Terms and Privacy

Appointment text messages sent through Apex Command Center · Last updated November 10, 2026

Program

This program sends appointment text messages for scheduling. When you book an appointment and check the box to agree, you receive a confirmation and reminders about that appointment from the business you scheduled with. Each text names that business and gives a phone number you can use to reach them. The messages are about your appointment only. No marketing messages are sent.

The service is provided by Resonate Business Systems LLC, which operates Apex Command Center.

How you agree

You agree on the booking page by checking the box next to the text message notice. The box is never checked for you. Agreeing to texts is not required to book an appointment.

Messages and rates

  • Message frequency varies with your appointments.
  • Message and data rates may apply.
  • Carriers are not liable for any delayed or undelivered messages.

Help and opting out

  • For help, reply HELP to any message, or write to support@resonateai.online.
  • To stop receiving messages, reply STOP to any message. You will get one message confirming that you are unsubscribed, and no more after that.

Privacy

We collect your name, your mobile phone number and your appointment details so the business can schedule you and send these messages. We use them only for that purpose.

We do not sell or share your SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes. Mobile information and text message opt-in consent are not shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

Our full Privacy Policy and Terms of Service also apply.
